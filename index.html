RetinaXAI: Explainable Retinal Disease Diagnostics

0.92 ROC-AUC (DME)

Multi-task deep learning pipeline for diabetic retinopathy (5-class severity) and DME (binary) detection, built on EfficientNet-B3 with dual classification heads. Uses Grad-CAM to generate visual saliency heatmaps for clinical interpretability, with a risk-triage output layer and Streamlit deployment. Trained and validated on the real-world IDRiD dataset.
